About the club

Olympic-Art is a Taekwondo club that trains children, youth and adults. Training is organised in age categories, and every athlete follows the belt progression at their own pace, from white through to black.

What the club keeps track of for every member:

  • Personal and contact details of the athlete
  • Training category and role in the group
  • Current belt level
  • Whether the membership is active or paused

All records on this site are stored in your own browser using Local Storage, so nothing is sent to a server.
